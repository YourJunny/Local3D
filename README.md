# Local3D

A free desktop app that turns a text prompt or a picture into a 3D model, with everything running on your own PC.

> **Status: in development.** There is no download yet. When the first build is ready it will appear on the [Releases page](https://github.com/YourJunny/Local3D/releases).

## What it will do

- **Text to 3D and image to 3D**, generated locally on your graphics card.
- **Free.** No account, no sign-in, no subscription, no paid tier.
- **Private.** Nothing you type or make is uploaded anywhere.
- **Export** to common 3D formats, game engines, Blender and 3D printing.

## What you will need

- Windows 10 or 11, or Linux
- An NVIDIA graphics card for generating models. Without one, the app still opens as a model library, viewer and export tool.

## Download

Downloads will be published on the [Releases page](https://github.com/YourJunny/Local3D/releases).

## License

Local3D is free to download and use. It is closed source: this repository holds the downloads and documentation, not the app's code.

The AI models the app can download each come with their own license from their makers. The app shows each model's terms next to its download button.
